How it works Trust

Good help is usually one recommendation away.

DoorServe is where neighbours ask for electricians, tailors, mechanics and home cooks, and where local providers build a name people can check. Starting in Kashmir.

How it works

  1. Post what you need

    Describe the job in your own words, or browse what neighbours are asking and sharing.

  2. Hear back

    Providers and neighbours reply in the comments. Like the answers that helped.

  3. Check the profile

    See who you’re dealing with, what they’ve posted and whether we’ve verified them.

A verified badge means we checked.

We never hand out the badge automatically. A provider shows it only after DoorServe has completed the verification we describe. Until then their profile says “Not verified yet”.
